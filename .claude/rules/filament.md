# Filament

When working with Filament (resources, pages, widgets, tables, forms, actions, schemas, infolists,
or their tests):

1. Follow the area rules for the path: `.ai/rules/filament.md`, `.ai/rules/resources.md`, and
   `.ai/rules/app-resources.md`.
2. Choose and configure components by `design-patterns.md`, and word them by `ui-text.md`.
3. Make list pages searchable by `list-search.md`.
4. Use Boost's `search-docs` for the installed Filament version's API before relying on memory;
   this app runs Filament v5, whose namespaces and signatures differ from earlier versions.
